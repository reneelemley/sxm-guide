Dear Friends & Family,

The first thing you need to know is to get your mind right the minute you step off the plane. You've arrived in paradise — but nothing here works quite right, and you won't see one moment of efficiency the whole time you're here.

If you can get into that headspace, this island will be the gift that keeps on giving.

Have fun. Be safe. And for the love of God, don't tip like an American.
